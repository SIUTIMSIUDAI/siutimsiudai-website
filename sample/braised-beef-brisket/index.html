Braised Beef Brisket with Radish 蘿蔔炆牛腩

  • Serves 4 · 4 人份
  • 90 minutes · 90 分鐘
  • Cantonese · 粵菜

One of two sample recipes written for the Siu Tim Siu Dai app, so the recipe import walkthrough has something to import. It is our own writing, not reproduced from any other cookbook or website.

Ingredients 材料

  • beef brisket / 牛腩 1 斤 (1 catty, about 605 g)
  • white radish / 白蘿蔔 2 個
  • ginger slices / 薑 4 片
  • spring onion / 葱 2 條
  • light soy sauce / 生抽 2 湯匙 (about 30 ml)
  • rock sugar / 冰糖 1 兩 (1 tael, about 38 g)
  • shaoxing wine / 紹興酒 2 湯匙 (about 30 ml)

Method 做法

  1. Blanch the brisket, then cut into bite-size cubes. 牛腩汆水，切件。
  2. Fry ginger and spring onion, return beef, splash in the wine. 爆香薑葱，回牛腩，灒酒。
  3. Add soy sauce, sugar and water to cover; braise on low. 加豉油、糖同水蓋過，細火炆。 Timer 60 min · 計時 60 分鐘
  4. Add radish chunks and braise until tender. 加蘿蔔件，炆至腍。 Timer 20 min · 計時 20 分鐘
