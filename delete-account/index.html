Delete your account 刪除帳戶

少甜少底 SiuTimSiuDai · iPhone and Android · Last updated: November 23, 2026 · 最後更新:2026 年 11 月 23 日

English

This page explains how to delete your 少甜少底 SiuTimSiuDai account, what we delete, and the few records that are kept.

Have a Pro or Max subscription? Cancel it first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it, so Apple or Google would keep charging you.

On Android, cancel in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open 少甜少底 SiuTimSiuDai and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and the app clears your data from that phone. This can't be undone.

Can't use the app? Email us

Email support@siutimsiudai.app from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. You don't need to install the app again.

To keep your account safe, we only act on requests sent from the account's own email address, and we may reply to check it's really you. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, say so in your email and we'll help you confirm which account is yours.

What we delete

  • Your account: your email address, sign-in details, display name and profile photo.
  • Your health details: your health profile (such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal), and the nutrition targets and weight history worked out from it.
  • Everything you've logged and saved: your meal logs, the meals in The usual, and your recipes, pantry, meal plans and shopping lists.
  • Your usage counts: the daily count of AI requests we keep to apply each plan's limits.
  • Your reports: any reports you've sent us about a person, a family, a recipe or a dish.
  • Your family links: if you manage a family on Max, the family is deleted too, along with its meal plans and invitations, and its members keep their own accounts and logs. If you're a family member, you leave the family, and its shared meal plans stay with the family.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase records: Apple or Google keep their own record of each purchase, which we can't delete. RevenueCat, the service that tells the app which plan you have, keeps the history of your purchases until you ask us to delete it. Just email us.
  • Crash reports: these never contain your account details, your diary or your photos, so they can't be traced back to you. Sentry, our crash-reporting service, deletes them automatically within 90 days.
  • Server logs: short-term technical logs, which can include your account's random ID, are deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Reports about you: if someone else reported you, their report stays for up to 12 months from when it was sent, so we can deal with it, but it no longer links to your account.

Data on your phone

Meal photos are only ever stored on your phone, never on our servers. Deleting your account in the app clears your data from that phone. If you used the app on another phone or tablet, or asked us by email, delete the app from those devices to remove what it stored there.

Keep your account, delete some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove data. In the app you can delete single meals, recipes and pantry items, remove your profile photo, or leave a family at any time.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data. Questions? Email support@siutimsiudai.app.

繁體中文

本頁說明如何刪除您的少甜少底 SiuTimSiuDai 帳戶、我們會刪除哪些資料,以及會保留的少量紀錄。

有 Pro 或 Max 訂閱?請先取消訂閱。刪除帳戶不會取消訂閱,Apple 或 Google 會繼續向您收費。

Android:於 Google Play 應用程式的「付款和訂閱」取消。iPhone:開啟「設定」,點按您的名稱,再選擇「訂閱項目」。

於應用程式內刪除

  1. 開啟少甜少底 SiuTimSiuDai,前往「我的」分頁。
  2. 捲動至最底,點按「刪除帳戶」。
  3. 再點按一次「刪除帳戶」確認。

您的帳戶及其資料會即時從我們的伺服器刪除,應用程式亦會清除該部手機上的資料。此操作無法復原。

無法使用應用程式?請電郵我們

請以您登入時所用的電郵地址,電郵至 support@siutimsiudai.app,要求刪除帳戶。您無需重新安裝應用程式。

為保障您的帳戶,我們只會處理由帳戶本身電郵地址發出的要求,並可能回覆以確認是您本人。我們會於 30 日內刪除帳戶及其資料,完成後會以電郵通知您。

如您以 Apple 登入並選擇了隱藏電郵地址,請在電郵中註明,我們會協助您確認哪個帳戶屬於您。

我們會刪除的資料

  • 您的帳戶:電郵地址、登入資料、顯示名稱及個人頭像。
  • 您的健康資料:健康檔案(例如年齡、性別、身高、體重、運動量及目標),以及據此計算的營養目標及體重紀錄。
  • 您記錄及儲存的所有內容:餐膳記錄、「老規矩」內的餐膳,以及您的食譜、存貨、餐膳計劃及購物清單。
  • 您的使用次數:我們為執行各計劃上限而記錄的每日人工智能使用次數。
  • 您的舉報:您曾向我們提交有關用戶、家庭、食譜或菜式的舉報。
  • 您的家庭連結:如您是 Max 家庭的管理者,家庭亦會一併刪除,包括其餐單及邀請,而家庭成員會保留各自的帳戶及記錄。如您是家庭成員,您會離開家庭,家庭共享的餐單則會保留在家庭內。

我們會保留的資料及保留期

  • 購買紀錄:Apple 或 Google 會保留其每次購買的紀錄,我們無法刪除。RevenueCat(讓應用程式得知您所屬計劃的服務)會保留您的購買紀錄,直至您要求我們刪除為止,電郵我們即可。
  • 當機報告:報告絕不包含您的帳戶資料、飲食記錄或相片,因此無法追溯至您本人。我們的當機報告服務 Sentry 會於 90 日內自動刪除這些報告。
  • 伺服器記錄:短期技術記錄可能包含您帳戶的隨機識別碼,會於 7 日內自動刪除。
  • 有關您的舉報:如有其他人舉報您,該舉報會由提交起保留最多 12 個月,以便我們處理,但不再連結至您的帳戶。

手機上的資料

餐膳相片只會儲存於您的手機,絕不會儲存於我們的伺服器。於應用程式內刪除帳戶,會清除該部手機上的資料。如您曾在其他手機或平板電腦使用本應用程式,或以電郵要求刪除,請在有關裝置上刪除應用程式,以移除其儲存的資料。

保留帳戶,只刪除部分資料

您無需刪除帳戶亦可移除資料。您可隨時於應用程式內刪除個別餐膳、食譜及存貨項目、移除個人頭像,或離開家庭。

有關我們如何處理您的資料,請參閱我們的私隱政策。如有疑問,請電郵 support@siutimsiudai.app。
