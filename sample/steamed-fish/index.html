Steamed Sea Bass 清蒸鱸魚

  • Serves 2 · 2 人份
  • 20 minutes · 20 分鐘
  • Cantonese · 粵菜

One of two sample recipes written for the Siu Tim Siu Dai app, so the recipe import walkthrough has something to import. It is our own writing, not reproduced from any other cookbook or website.

呢個係為少甜少底應用程式而寫嘅兩個示範食譜之一,用嚟示範食譜匯入功能。內容由我哋自己撰寫,並非轉載自其他食譜書或網站。

Ingredients 材料

  • sea bass / 鱸魚 1 條
  • ginger / 薑 5 片
  • spring onion / 葱 2 條
  • steamed-fish soy / 蒸魚豉油 2 湯匙 (about 30 ml)
  • hot oil / 熱油 1 湯匙 (about 15 ml)

Method 做法

  1. Score the fish, lay ginger underneath and on top. 魚身別花，薑墊底鋪面。
  2. Steam over high heat until just cooked. 大火蒸至剛熟。 Timer 8 min · 計時 8 分鐘
  3. Top with spring onion, pour over hot oil and soy. 鋪葱，淋熱油同豉油。
